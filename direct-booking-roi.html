What does this calculator measure?

This calculator estimates the financial value of direct hotel bookings generated through advertising.

How hotel ROAS is calculated

ROAS is attributed booking revenue divided by advertising spend. For example, $50,000 in booking revenue from $10,000 in advertising spend produces a 5.0× ROAS.

Why compare direct bookings with OTA bookings?

Direct bookings generally avoid OTA commission. Comparing estimated OTA commission with direct-booking variable costs helps illustrate the potential distribution value of direct demand.

What is cost per booking?

Cost per booking divides advertising spend by bookings attributed to that spend.

How should I use the results?

Treat the output as a planning model rather than a profit-and-loss statement.
