What is hotel ROAS?

Hotel ROAS, or return on ad spend, measures how much attributed booking revenue is generated for each dollar spent on advertising. A 5.0× ROAS means $5 in attributed booking revenue for every $1 in ad spend.

How is hotel ROAS calculated?

Divide attributed booking revenue by advertising spend. If a hotel spends $10,000 and attributes $50,000 in booking revenue to those campaigns, ROAS is 5.0×.

What is a good hotel ROAS?

There is no universal target. The right ROAS depends on contribution margin, cancellation rates, channel mix, brand strategy, incrementality, and whether the campaign is acquiring new demand or capturing demand that already existed.

Why cost per booking matters

Cost per booking can be easier to compare across campaigns and properties than ROAS alone, especially when average booking values differ.
