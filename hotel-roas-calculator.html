Hotel ROAS Calculator FAQ

How do you calculate hotel ROAS?

Divide attributed booking revenue by advertising spend.

Is a higher ROAS always better?

A higher ROAS indicates more attributed revenue per advertising dollar, but profitability and incrementality also matter.

What is the difference between ROAS and ROI?

ROAS compares advertising revenue with advertising spend. ROI typically considers a broader set of costs and returns.

Hotel ROAS formula and example

ROAS = Attributed booking revenue ÷ Advertising spend. Spending $2,000 on ads that generate $10,000 in attributed booking revenue yields 5× ROAS.

ROAS is not profit: account for cancellations, payment costs, room operating expenses and attribution limitations. Compare with Direct Booking ROI and OTA commission costs.

Hotel ROAS FAQs

Is a 5× ROAS profitable?

Not necessarily. Profitability depends on costs, margins and attribution accuracy.

Does ROAS include cancellations?

Adjust attributed booking revenue for cancellations and refunds where practical.
