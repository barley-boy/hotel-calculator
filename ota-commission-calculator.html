How to calculate OTA commission

Multiply OTA booking revenue by the commission percentage. If a hotel generates $100,000 in OTA booking revenue at an 18% commission rate, estimated commission expense is $18,000.

What is an OTA commission?

Online travel agencies commonly earn a percentage of the booking value in exchange for distribution, demand generation, merchandising, and booking services. Actual commercial terms vary by hotel, market, program, and platform.

Why compare OTA and direct booking costs?

The comparison can help illustrate distribution economics. It should not be interpreted as proof that OTA demand could have been acquired directly at the same volume or cost.
