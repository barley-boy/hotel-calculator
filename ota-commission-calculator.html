OTA Commission Calculator FAQ

How is OTA commission calculated?

Multiply eligible OTA booking revenue by the applicable commission rate.

Can every OTA booking be shifted direct?

No. OTA demand may be incremental, so commission savings should not assume every OTA booking would otherwise book direct.

Why compare OTA and direct distribution costs?

The comparison helps hotel teams understand channel economics and the potential value of direct demand.
